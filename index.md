# Welcome to My ePortfolio
---
This portfolio represents the culmination of my bachelor's degree in Computer Science with a software engineering focus at Southern New Hampshire University. Throughout this program, I’ve gained exposure to a wide range of languages, tools, and platforms that form the cornerstone of my technical foundation. I have focused on writing maintainable code, secure design, and scalable architecture. This capstone project brings those strengths together in a cohesive, production-ready application.

My goal with this portfolio is simple:  
**Show how I think, how I build, and how I improve through iteration.**

Each enhancement demonstrates a different dimension of my engineering approach:

- **Software Design & Engineering** — intentional architecture, modularity, and maintainability  
- **Algorithms & Data Structures** — efficient data handling and improved performance  
- **Databases & Security** — secure coding practices, validation, and robust data modeling  

This site walks through each enhancement, the original artifact they are based on, the improvements I made, and the reasoning behind every technical decision.
